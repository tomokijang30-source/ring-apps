Frequently asked questions

Why is the estimate different from my payslip?

By default the App uses the general statutory rules of your country and work setup. Public-sector rules, averaged-hours schemes, collective agreements and company rules can differ. The amounts are pre-tax estimates for guidance only, not legal advice. Open Pay settings and change the hours and percentages to match your contract.

I bought Pro but it is not showing. How do I cancel?

Tap Settings > Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy. To cancel a subscription, open Settings > Manage Subscription (or iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions). You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Will my shifts carry over to a new iPhone?

Yes, if you move to the new iPhone from an iCloud Backup or a computer backup (or with Quick Start): the App's data is part of that backup. You can also save a backup file (Settings > Backup > Save) and open it in the App on the new iPhone. We keep no copy of your data, so without either one it cannot be recovered.
